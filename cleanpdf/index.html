CleanPDF

Cleans a web page of ads, pop-ups and cookie banners, then opens Chrome's print dialog to save it as PDF. Works locally.

What it does

  • Removes ads, cookie and consent banners, newsletter pop-ups and sticky overlays, and keeps the article.
  • Opens Chrome's print dialog on the cleaned page, where you choose “Save as PDF” (A4 or Letter), with selectable, searchable text.
  • Free: 5 saves a day.
  • Pro ($4.99 once, or $1.99/month; 7-day free trial): unlimited saves, click-to-remove, header/footer, file name templates, Markdown/HTML export and a dark-page fix.

Privacy

Pages are processed on your computer and never uploaded. No analytics or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Buying Pro, cancelling, refunds
