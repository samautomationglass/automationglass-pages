CleanPDF

Save any web page as a clean PDF without ads, pop-ups or cookie banners. One click, processed locally in your browser.

What it does

  • Removes ads, cookie and consent banners, newsletter pop-ups and sticky overlays, and keeps the article.
  • Saves the PDF straight to your Downloads folder (A4 or Letter), with selectable, searchable text.
  • Free: 5 saves a day.
  • Pro ($4.99 once, or $1.99/month; 7-day free trial): unlimited saves, all tabs as one PDF, click-to-remove, header/footer, file name templates, Markdown/HTML export and a dark-page fix.

Privacy

Pages are processed on your computer and never uploaded. No analytics or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Buying Pro, cancelling, refunds

Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (payments by Stripe). See the terms of sale and the refund policy. To cancel a subscription, open CleanPDF and click “Manage or cancel subscription”.
