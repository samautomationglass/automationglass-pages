CleanPDF – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the CleanPDF Chrome extension (“CleanPDF”, “the extension”) · This page: https://samautomationglass.github.io/automationglass-pages/cleanpdf/privacy.html

Summary

CleanPDF does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your data. The pages you save are processed on your computer and the files go to your Downloads folder. CleanPDF has no analytics, tracking or telemetry of any kind. The only network requests the extension itself makes go to ExtensionPay, to check whether you have paid for Pro (or started a free trial), and only after you have opened its upgrade, trial or log-in page.

What the extension does with web pages

When you click the CleanPDF button, the extension reads and changes the page in your current tab (or, with Pro “save all tabs”, the tabs in your current window) to remove ads and pop-ups. It reads the page's title and address only to name the file. It then makes a PDF, Markdown or HTML file and saves it to your Downloads folder. All of this happens in your browser. Page content, page addresses (URLs) and your browsing history are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The optional “debugger” permission is used only to create the PDF file with Chrome's built-in Page.printToPDF command (and, for Pro “save all tabs”, to run CleanPDF's own packaged clean-up script in each tab). It is not used to read network traffic, cookies, passwords or form data. If you decline it, CleanPDF uses Chrome's print dialog instead.

Removing the extension deletes the data it stored on your device. When you remove it, Chrome opens a short thank-you page on this website (uninstalled.html); no information about you is added to that address. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement; the developer does not use them for tracking.

Payments and free trial (ExtensionPay and Stripe)

CleanPDF Pro is sold through ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe to process payments.

  • When you click “Upgrade to Pro”, “Try Pro free for 7 days”, “Already paid? Log in” or “Manage or cancel subscription”, ExtensionPay's page opens. Anything you enter there, such as your email address and card details, goes to ExtensionPay and Stripe, not to the extension.
  • After that, the extension contacts extensionpay.com with its random licence key to check whether you have paid or are in your trial. That request contains no page content, URLs or browsing data.
  • If you never open one of those pages, the extension makes no network requests at all.
  • The developer can see, in the ExtensionPay dashboard, the email address and payment or trial status of people who have paid or started a trial. The developer never sees card numbers. This information is used only to provide Pro and to answer support requests.

See the privacy policies of ExtensionPay and Stripe for how they handle your data.

What CleanPDF does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no use of data for anything other than saving pages and providing Pro.
  • No remote code: everything the extension runs is included in the package reviewed by the Chrome Web Store.

Children

CleanPDF is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
