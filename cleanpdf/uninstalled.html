Paying for Pro?

Removing the extension does not cancel a monthly subscription. A monthly plan ($1.99/month) keeps renewing until it is cancelled. To cancel it:

  1. Reinstall CleanPDF from the Chrome Web Store (it's free).
  2. Click the CleanPDF button in Chrome's toolbar to open its popup.
  3. If Pro isn't shown as active, click “Already paid? Log in” and enter the email address you paid with, then follow the link ExtensionPay emails you.
  4. Click “Manage or cancel subscription” and cancel on the ExtensionPay page. You can then remove CleanPDF again.

Or simply email support@automationglass.com from the address you paid with, and we'll cancel it for you. After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for and is not renewed.

One-time (lifetime) licence? If you paid $4.99 once, there is nothing to cancel: it was a single payment and you will never be charged again.
