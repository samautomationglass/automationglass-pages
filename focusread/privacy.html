FocusRead – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Applies to the FocusRead Chrome extension (“FocusRead”, “the extension”).

Summary

FocusRead does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your data. The pages you read are restyled on your computer and nothing about them leaves your browser. The only network requests the extension itself makes go to ExtensionPay, to check whether you have paid for Pro, and only after you have opened its upgrade or log-in page.

What the extension does with web pages

FocusRead runs on the web pages you open and changes how their text looks: it makes the start of each word bold, and for Pro users it can add a reading ruler and switch the text to the OpenDyslexic font. It skips code, form fields and editable areas, and does not read what you type. All of this happens in your browser. Page content, page addresses (URLs) and your browsing history are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings (on/off, bold amount, sites where FocusRead is off, and Pro looks, presets and allow/deny lists) and a cached Pro licence answer (paid or not, and when it was checked), in Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device.
  • If you are a Pro user and turn on sync, your settings are also saved in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome syncs through your own Google account to your other computers. FocusRead's developer has no access to it.
  • Licence information from ExtensionPay (a random licence key, whether you have paid, and the install date), in Chrome's extension storage.

Removing the extension deletes the data it stored on your device.

Payments (ExtensionPay and Stripe)

FocusRead Pro is sold through ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe to process payments.

  • When you click “Get Pro” or “Already paid?”, ExtensionPay's page opens. Anything you enter there, such as your email address and card details, goes to ExtensionPay and Stripe, not to the extension.
  • After that, the extension contacts extensionpay.com with its random licence key to check whether you have paid. That request contains no page content, URLs or browsing data.
  • If you never open the upgrade or log-in page, the extension makes no network requests at all.
  • The developer can see, in the ExtensionPay dashboard, the email address and payment status of people who have paid. The developer never sees card numbers. This information is used only to provide Pro and to answer support requests.

See the privacy policies of ExtensionPay and Stripe for how they handle your data.

What FocusRead does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no use of data for anything other than restyling text and providing Pro.
  • No remote code: everything the extension runs, including the font, is included in the package reviewed by the Chrome Web Store.

Children

FocusRead is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
