14-day refund, no questions asked

If NightSheets Pro is not right for you, email support@automationglass.com within 14 days of a payment (the first purchase or a yearly renewal) from, or mentioning, the email address you paid with. We will refund that payment in full to the original payment method. You don't need to give a reason.

  • Refunds are issued through ExtensionPay/Stripe; banks usually take 5–10 business days to show them.
  • After a refund, Pro is switched off for that purchase. The free features keep working.
  • A refunded yearly plan is also cancelled, so it does not renew.
  • After 14 days, we still consider requests case by case, for example if a Google change broke a Pro feature and we could not fix it.

The free trial

The 1-day trial costs nothing and needs no card, so there is nothing to refund. It ends by itself.

Cancelling instead

To stop a yearly plan from renewing without a refund, open NightSheets options and click Manage or cancel subscription, or email us. See the terms of sale.
