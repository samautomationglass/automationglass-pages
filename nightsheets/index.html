NightSheets

A Chrome extension that shows Google Sheets, Docs and Slides in a dark theme.

  • Free: dark mode for Sheets, Docs and Slides, a switch for each app, and the Alt+Shift+D shortcut.
  • Pro ($9 a year or $19.99 once, with a 1-day free trial): contrast presets (Soft, High contrast, AAA low-vision, Warm tint), a different preset per app, and a schedule (custom hours or sunset to sunrise).
  • Styling only: your files, printing and exports are not changed. No analytics or tracking.
