Paying for Pro?

Removing the extension does not cancel a yearly Pro plan. A yearly plan ($9/year) keeps renewing until it is cancelled. To cancel it:

  1. Reinstall NightSheets from the Chrome Web Store (it's free).
  2. Click the NightSheets button in Chrome's toolbar to open its popup (or open its options page).
  3. If Pro isn't shown as active, click Already paid? Log in and enter the email address you paid with, then follow the link ExtensionPay emails you.
  4. Click Manage or cancel subscription and cancel on the ExtensionPay page. You can then remove NightSheets again.

Or simply email support@automationglass.com from the address you paid with, and we will cancel it for you. After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not renewed.

Lifetime licence or free trial? If you paid $19.99 once, there is nothing to cancel: it was a single payment and you will never be charged again. The 1-day free trial needs no card and ends by itself.

Within 14 days of a payment you can also ask for a full refund, no questions asked (refund policy).
